Community Care Protocols

How to Use Pharmacy Services

Learn how to navigate our pharmacy resources efficiently. Follow this structured roadmap to receive professional guidance, choose appropriate over-the-counter support, and obtain rapid first-aid assistance.

  1. General Medicine Guidance

    Bring your current prescriptions or medicine boxes to the counter. Our pharmacists explain active ingredient names, verify expiry dates, clarify dosing frequencies, and warn against potential food-drug or drug-drug interactions before you start any regimen.

    Active Ingredients Interaction Screening Storage Best Practices
    Browse Common Meds

  2. Over-The-Counter (OTC) Consultation

    Discuss mild, self-limiting health symptoms—such as seasonal allergic sneezes, mild tension headaches, superficial burns, or acute indigestion. The pharmacist assesses whether home management is safe or if red-flag symptoms necessitate an immediate physician referral.

    Symptom Assessment Red-Flag Triage Non-Prescription Safety
    Inquire With Desk

  3. Basic Wellness Checks

    Take advantage of routine non-invasive vital checks conducted in our private consultation pod. Monitor resting blood pressure, pulse oximetry, body temperature, and BMI indices. Our team logs baseline readings to help you maintain chronic disease awareness.

    Blood Pressure Screening Oxygen Saturation Temperature Logs
    Preventative Health

  4. First-Aid Support & Emergency Kit Guidance

    Access rapid assistance for minor scrapes, sterile wound antiseptic cleansing, and non-emergent dressings. We also offer individualized recommendations on stocking comprehensive family and travel first-aid kits tailored to students, children, or elderly household members.

    Sterile Dressing Minor Burn Relief Emergency Kit Assembly
    Rapid Response Ready
